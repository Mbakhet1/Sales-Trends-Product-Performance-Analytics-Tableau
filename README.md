# Sales-Trends-Product-Performance-Analytics-Tableau
Sales Trends & Product Performance Analytics
Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying growth opportunities and operational inefficiencies within a retail environment through interactive data visualization. Using Tableau, I developed a comprehensive dashboard that tracks key sales metrics over time, allowing stakeholders to identify seasonal peaks and evaluate the success of specific product lines.

Visualizing the Insights
Below are the key views from the interactive dashboard.

1. Executive Sales Overview
This view provides a high-level summary of total revenue, profit margins, and year-over-year growth.

2. Seasonal Trend Analysis
This visualization highlights sales peaks and troughs across different fiscal quarters to inform better inventory planning.

3. Product Performance Matrix
A detailed breakdown of product success based on sales volume and profitability.

Key Business Questions Addressed
Which product categories are driving the highest percentage of total revenue?

How does sales volume fluctuate across different fiscal quarters (Seasonality)?

Are there specific regions or categories that are underperforming relative to historical averages?

Technical Implementation
Calculated Fields: Implemented custom formulas to derive growth rates and profit margins.

Dynamic Filtering: Integrated interactive filters for category and date ranges to allow for granular exploration.

UI/UX Design: Applied dashboard design principles to ensure a clean, scannable layout for business stakeholders.

Recommendations
Inventory Optimization: Use identified sales peaks to adjust stock levels ahead of high-demand quarters.

Strategic Marketing: Allocate additional budget to high-performing categories identified in the performance matrix.

How to View This Project
